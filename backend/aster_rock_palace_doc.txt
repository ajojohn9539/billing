1. Overview
This document describes how the Rock Palace accounting software works: its modules,
screens, fields, rules and reports. It covers every menu in the top navigation: Contacts,
Products, Purchase, Sales, Damage Entry, Stock, Others, Daybook.
Goals
Record every purchase, sale, payment and receipt of the shop.
Track stone stock (slabs, tiles, blocks) accurately, including damage.
Know customer dues and supplier payables at any time.
Give a daily cash/bank view (Day Book) and profit reports.

2. General Rules (apply to all modules)
1. Add – Click Add/New, fill the form, press Save. Required fields are marked and
validated before saving.
2. Edit – Open the record from the list (pencil icon), change fields, press Update. Every
edit stores who changed it and when.

3. Delete – Uses soft delete (record is hidden, not erased). A record cannot be deleted if
it is linked to other records (e.g. a product that has stock or sales). Delete asks for
confirmation and is allowed for Admin only.
4. Search, sort, paginate – Every list has a Search box, sortable columns and a Show N
entries selector (as on the Day Book screen).
5. Dates are shown as dd/mm/yyyy. Time is recorded automatically.
6. Currency is Indian Rupee (₹), shown as 0/- .
7. Audit – Created by, updated by, created at and updated at are stored on every table.
8. Stock and account balances are always recalculated automatically when a purchase,
sale, return or damage is added, edited or deleted.

3. Contacts
Manages customers, suppliers and (optionally) staff/others.
Fields: Contact type (Customer / Supplier / Both), Name, Company, Phone, Email,
Address, City, State, GSTIN, Opening balance (and whether To Receive or To Pay), Credit
limit, Notes, Status (Active/Inactive).
Actions
Add: Contacts → Add Contact → fill form → Save.
Edit: change details; opening balance can be edited only before any transaction exists.
Delete: blocked if the contact has purchases, sales or daybook entries; mark Inactive
instead.
Ledger: shows all bills, payments, and current balance (Due / Excess) for the contact.

4. Products
Stone items sold or bought by Rock Palace.
Fields: Product name (e.g. Black Galaxy Granite, Italian Marble, Kota Stone), Code/SKU,
Category (Granite, Marble, Tiles, Slab, Block, Others), Sub category, Unit (sq.ft, sq.m,
piece, ton, cubic ft), Size / thickness, Purchase price, Selling price, Tax (GST %), HSN code,
Opening stock, Minimum stock (reorder level), Image, Status.
Actions
Add / Edit: from Products → Add Product. Changing price does not alter old bills.

On Save the system
1. Creates the purchase and its items.
2. Increases stock of each product.
3. Adds the balance to the supplier's payable.
4. If an amount is paid, creates an Outward entry in the Day Book (cash/bank).

5.3 Edit Purchase
Open the purchase → Edit → change items, rates, quantity, supplier or payment.
On Update the system reverses the old stock and account effects and applies the
new ones.
Not allowed if the edit would make stock negative (e.g. goods already sold). A clear
error is shown.

5.4 Delete Purchase
Admin only, with confirmation.
Reverses stock and supplier balance; linked payments in the Day Book are also
removed (or must be removed first, as configured).
Blocked if stock from this purchase has already been sold and deleting would cause
negative stock.

5.5 Purchase payment and return
Add payment: records a part/full payment against a purchase (creates an Outward
Day Book entry).
Purchase return: returns goods to the supplier; reduces stock and the payable (or
creates a refund due).

6. Sales
Sales list: Sl.No., Date, Bill No., Customer, Total, Received, Due, Status, Actions.
Add Sale
Header: Customer (or walk-in), Date, Bill number (auto), Payment method, Vehicle no. /
delivery address (for stone transport), Notes.
Items: Product, Quantity (cannot exceed available stock), Rate (defaults to selling
price, editable by permitted roles), Discount, Tax, Total.
Footer: Sub total, Discount, Tax, Transport charges, Round off, Grand total, Amount
received, Balance due.
On Save: reduces stock, adds to customer receivable, and creates an Inward Day Book
entry for the amount received.
Edit / Delete follow the same reverse-and-reapply logic as Purchase. Deleted bills are
soft-deleted and kept in the audit log.
Other actions: Print / PDF invoice, Add payment (receipt), Sales return, Quotation to Bill
conversion.

7. Damage Entry
Used when stone is broken, cracked or otherwise unsellable.
Fields: Date, Product, Quantity, Reason (Broken in transit / Cutting loss / Handling /
Other), Cost value (auto = purchase price × quantity), Remarks, Photo (optional).
Effects: Reduces stock; the cost value is recorded as a loss in reports.
Edit / Delete: Edit restores the old quantity and applies the new one. Delete restores the
stock. Admin approval recommended.

8. Stock
Read-only views calculated from purchases, sales, returns and damages.
Stock summary: product, unit, opening, purchased, sold, damaged, current stock,
stock value.
Low stock report: items below minimum stock.
Stock ledger: every movement of one product with date and reference.

10. Others
Expenses: rent, electricity, salary, fuel, loading/unloading (posted through Day Book).
Bank accounts: add accounts, opening balance, transfers between cash and bank.
Categories & Sub categories for income/expense.
Reports: Sales report, Purchase report, Profit & Loss, Customer dues, Supplier
payables, GST summary, Stock valuation, Day-wise cash book. Export to PDF/Excel.
Settings: company profile (Rock Palace name, logo, address, GSTIN), invoice prefix and
numbering, tax rates, user roles, backup.
Users: create staff logins and assign roles.

12. Suggested Laravel Structure
Models: Contact, Product, Category, Purchase, PurchaseItem, Sale, SaleItem, Damage,
StockMovement, BankAccount, Daybook.
Routes: Route::resource('purchases', PurchaseController::class) gives index,
create, store, show, edit, update, destroy. Same for sales, products, contacts, damages.
Use DB transactions in store, update and destroy so stock and accounts never go out
of sync.
Validation: Form Request classes for each module.
Authorization: Policies / roles for edit and delete.
Soft deletes and an audit log observer on all models.

13. Daily Workflow
1. Morning: check Day Book opening balance and low stock.

2. Add purchases when stone arrives; record supplier payment.
3. Create sales bills; record customer receipts.
4. Enter damages and expenses as they happen.
5. Evening: review Day Book daily balance, customer dues and close the day.
6. Weekly/monthly: run reports, take a database backup.

14. Security and Backup
Password-protected login with role-based permissions.
Daily automatic database backup; keep copies off-site.
Delete and price-edit actions restricted and logged.
